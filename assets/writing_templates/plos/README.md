# PLOS manuscript starters

These are independently written Quill starters (MIT), not copies of the
complete publisher template and not publisher-endorsed submission checks.
They provide a single-column, double-spaced manuscript with continuous line
numbers. Computational Biology and Genetics also include an author summary.
Check the target journal's current requirements before submission.

The unmodified `plos2025.bst` is included from the official PLOS template package
downloaded on 17 September 2026. Its header permits redistribution under
LPPL 1.3 or later. The licence and original copyright notices are retained.
The full publisher template PDF and sample manuscript are not redistributed.

Official package: https://journals.plos.org/plosone/s/file?id=1457%2FPLOS_latex_template.zip
LaTeX guidance: https://journals.plos.org/plosone/s/latex
Computational Biology: https://journals.plos.org/ploscompbiol/s/submission-guidelines
Genetics: https://journals.plos.org/plosgenetics/s/submission-guidelines

Use References to sync your personal library to `references.bib`. This preserves
the supplied PLOS bibliography style. Figures and other submission files should
be prepared according to the target journal's instructions.
